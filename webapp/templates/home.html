Phishing link checker

Check a link before you open it.

PhishLens reads only the text of a URL, never the page behind it, and tells you whether it looks like phishing.

Try ···

01How it works
  1. Read the link. Only the characters of the URL are used. The page is never opened, so nothing can load or track you.
  2. Measure 36 signals. Length, randomness, bait words, brand names and look-alike spellings, such as rn standing in for m.
  3. Decide and explain. A gradient boosted model scores the link, and SHAP shows which signals pushed that score up or down.
02Three answers
Phishing
Clear signs of deception, such as a misspelled brand or bait words.
Legitimate
Nothing suspicious in how the link is built.
Uncertain
Too little evidence, as with a shortened link. It says so instead of guessing.
03The report

Every check comes with a PDF you can preview, download and share. It covers why the model decided, the SHAP contributions, all 36 signals, and the domain's registration and certificate.
